01 — ABOUT

Learning meets behavior.

I am affiliated with the Law and Policy Lab in the Department of AI at GIST. My research interests focus on LLM fine-tuning and sycophancy.

I am interested in how language models are adapted and how their responses relate to the beliefs and preferences expressed by users.
